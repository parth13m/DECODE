## Abstract

This RFC defines the structural and behavioral contract of Decode's atomic knowledge unit, independent of terminology. It specifies required fields (id, subject, predicate, value, tier, provenance, confidence, grounding, version, status), identity semantics, versioning model, lifecycle states, composition rules, validation and invalidation contracts, and query semantics.

## Decision

The atomic unit contract was accepted and incorporated into DAS-002 (Decode Intermediate Representation) as the "Atomic Unit Contract" section.
